# Joining a queue on your phone

The kiosk's department screen displays a QR code for web-based queue entry. No
mobile app or paper ticket is required for this option.

1. Open the phone's camera and scan the QR code on the kiosk.
2. Open the link shown by the camera. It loads the service queue in the phone's
   web browser.
3. The web page automatically joins the department's queue and displays the
   department name and your queue number.
4. Keep the page open to see live status updates. While your ticket is waiting,
   tap **LEAVE QUEUE** if you need to cancel it.

The kiosk still offers a paper ticket as a fallback. An internet connection is
required to join and refresh queue status. Tickets can only be cancelled while
they are still waiting.
